Demucs model weights (htdemucs / 955717e8) - not included
=========================================================

KWAK Split packages do not contain Demucs model weights. On the first Split
Stems, the bundled Demucs 4.0.1 runtime downloads this checkpoint directly
from the official Demucs server and stores it in the app's models/torch
folder:

  https://dl.fbaipublicfiles.com/demucs/hybrid_transformer/955717e8-8726e21a.th
  84,141,911 bytes
  SHA-256: 8726e21a993978c7ba086d3872e7608d7d5bfca646ca4aca459ffda844faa8b4

Demucs checks the download against the hash prefix in its file name.

The weights are not part of KWAK Split and are not covered by its MIT
license. The Demucs author has stated that the model weights are not covered
by the MIT license of the Demucs code and are provided for research purposes:

  https://github.com/facebookresearch/demucs/issues/327#issuecomment-1134828611
  https://github.com/facebookresearch/demucs/issues/508#issuecomment-1663033356

KWAK Split does not redistribute the weights and grants no rights to them.
Before using Split Stems, check that your use is permitted by the model's
terms. You are also responsible for the rights to the audio you process.
