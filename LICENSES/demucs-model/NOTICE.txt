HTDemucs 955717e8 - historical MIT declaration and model attribution
=================================================================

KWAK Split uses the htdemucs / 955717e8 model from Demucs, developed by
Alexandre Defossez and the Demucs contributors. The upstream Demucs copyright
notice and complete MIT text are preserved unchanged in MIT-LICENSE.txt.
That text was obtained from the upstream Demucs code distribution; the specific
evidence for the model weights is the historical official model card below.

The included model's weights and declared model configuration were verified to
match the official artifact at:
https://huggingface.co/adefossez/HTDemucs/tree/bf35a81b663819a8255c8fefee17f9d812b786b5

That revision's model card expressly identifies the htdemucs weights and
955717e8.safetensors, and declares "license: mit". A verbatim copy is retained
as evidence/historical-model-card.md. All 533 tensors (41,984,456 elements),
their shapes and dtypes, and the model constructor configuration were compared.
The tensor payloads are byte-identical despite the different file formats.

Included original checkpoint:

  SHA-256: 8726e21a993978c7ba086d3872e7608d7d5bfca646ca4aca459ffda844faa8b4
Official historical safetensors artifact:
  SHA-256: d9fa14133cfcc034a6758923bb3a8ca9f8dfd0b582134643bbf83f72c17576dd

The owner states that this model was obtained and used while it was MIT
licensed. This is the owner's account; a specific historical download date
has not been independently established, and no such date is asserted.

The author later removed the MIT metadata without changing those weights.
The project relies on that historical official MIT declaration as the license
basis for this exact model. Retain this attribution, MIT-LICENSE.txt and the
historical declaration with copies of the model. This records the project's
reliance on an existing declaration, not a new permission by KWAK Split or
a new approval by the rights holder. See PROVENANCE.md for the full history,
and evidence.json for preserved-file hashes and exhaustive comparison results.
The project distribution decision is recorded in ../distribution-status.json.
