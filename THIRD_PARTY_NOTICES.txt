Stem separation model
---------------------

KWAK Split packages do not include Demucs model weights. On the first Split
Stems, the bundled Demucs runtime downloads the htdemucs checkpoint
955717e8-8726e21a.th from the official Demucs server into the app's models
folder:
https://dl.fbaipublicfiles.com/demucs/hybrid_transformer/955717e8-8726e21a.th
SHA-256: 8726e21a993978c7ba086d3872e7608d7d5bfca646ca4aca459ffda844faa8b4

The weights are not covered by KWAK Split's MIT license. The Demucs author has
stated that they are not covered by the Demucs MIT license and are provided for
research purposes. KWAK Split grants no rights to them; check that your use is
permitted. See LICENSES/demucs-model/NOTICE.txt and PROVENANCE.md.
