01 · THE BASICS
Is KWAK Split really free?+–
Yes — free forever. No trial timer, no watermark, no locked "pro" features, no card. Download it and it's yours.
Which platforms does it run on?+–
Windows 10 / 11 (64-bit). There is no macOS version.
Do I need internet or a fancy GPU?+–
Internet only for your first split, which downloads the Demucs model (about 84 MB) once. No GPU needed — splitting runs on your CPU, and any modern 64-bit machine with 4 GB of RAM handles it. A faster chip just means faster splits.
02 · STEMS & QUALITY
How many stems do I get?+–
Four: vocal, drums, bass, and "other" (everything else). Each lands on its own fader with mute and solo, so you can rebuild the mix however you want.
How good is the separation?+–
Clean enough to prompt with. Bleed depends on the source — dense, heavily-mastered tracks are harder than sparse ones — but for covers, remix prompts, and instrumentals it holds up nicely.
Can I get just the vocal or just the instrumental?+–
Yep. Solo the vocal, or mute it for an instrumental, then export. Stem Mixer → Export also saves all four stems as separate files.
03 · EXPORT & AI TOOLS
What files can I load and export?+–
Load WAV, MP3, FLAC, or M4A. Export WAV for lossless quality or MP3 for something small and fast. Solo a stem to export it on its own, or dump all four at once.
Does it work with Suno / Udio?+–
That's the whole point. Export a clean stem or instrumental, upload it as your reference or input, and generate. Isolate the element you care about, EQ out the mud, keep it dry — cleaner in, cleaner out.
Is my audio uploaded anywhere?+–
Never. There's no cloud step and no telemetry on your files. Every split, EQ move, and export happens locally on your machine.
04 · TROUBLESHOOTING & LICENSE
Windows warns about an unknown publisher+–
Click More info → Run anyway. SmartScreen flags new apps that aren't code-signed — nothing's wrong with the build. Download it only from the official GitHub release and compare the SHA-256 listed there if you want to be sure.
A split is slow or crashed on a huge file+–
It's CPU-bound, so big or long files take longer. Close heavy apps, or select a range and split just that section instead of the whole track. If a specific file keeps crashing, send us the details on the Contact page.
Where can I find the license terms?+–
KWAK Split's original code is covered by the MIT license. Included libraries have their own component notices, and the complete FFmpeg and LAME source is attached to each GitHub release. The Demucs model isn't included: your first split downloads it from the official Demucs server. Its weights are provided by their authors for research purposes and aren't covered by KWAK Split's license; see the model notice. Use audio you own or have permission to edit; processing a recording does not grant rights to its underlying content.
